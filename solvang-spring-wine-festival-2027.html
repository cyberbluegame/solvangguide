Why May in Solvang tastes like spring ☀️

A public wine-and-food day in Solvang Park — not a collapsed FAQ, not a hidden schedule. Read the whole pour list below.

Solvang Spring Wine & Food Festival 2027 is the village’s May tasting day: Santa Ynez wineries set up under the oaks at Solvang Park on Mission Drive, chefs plate cheese and strawberries, and a small stage keeps the afternoon moving. 🎶 The planning date is Saturday, May 8, 2027 — the same sunny weekend the valley also hosts the wine-country half marathon. If you are here for the pours, not the 13.1, this page is your map.

What makes it special is the scale. You can walk from a Danish bakery to a pinot pour in three minutes, sit in park shade, and still make a Los Olivos tasting room before dinner. 🌿 It is not March’s Taste of Solvang culinary weekend and it is not September’s heritage parades. It is spring in the Santa Ynez Valley: longer light, vineyard green, and a park that smells like oak and wine.

🎟️ Wristbands and walkable rooms disappear first. Lock a hotel near Solvang Park, then grab a tasting pass when the 2027 list opens.

Fully open festival schedule

No accordions. No “read more.” Times are Pacific Time and may shift when official 2027 wristbands publish.

☀️ Friday, May 7 — Check-in eve

  • 3:00 PM – 6:00 PM

    🎟️ Early wristband pickup

    If the 2027 pass offers Friday pickup, use Solvang Park or the Visitor Center on Mission Drive. Skipping the Saturday morning line is the whole point of arriving a night early.

  • 6:00 PM – 9:00 PM

    🧀 Village dinner & bakery boxes

    Book a table near Copenhagen Drive. Pick up kringle for Saturday breakfast so you are not in a pastry queue at 10:45 AM when gates are about to open.

🍷 Saturday, May 8 — Solvang Park tasting day

  • 10:00 AM

    🚶‍♀️ Walk in from Alisal lots

    Park once on the Alisal Road edge or behind Mission Drive. Copenhagen Drive is already a race-and-festival snarl by late morning. The park lawn is two blocks; wear comfortable shoes.

  • 11:00 AM

    🥂 Gates & first pours

    Wristbands on. Start with a crisp Santa Ynez chardonnay or rosé while the sun is still kind. ☀️ Water stations first if you drove; spit cups exist for a reason.

  • 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM

    🧀 Cheese, berries & chef plates

    Valley cheese boards, strawberry plates 🍓, and small chef bites at the food tents. This is the lunch window — eat before the 2:00 PM pinot rush.

  • 12:30 PM – 4:30 PM

    🎶 Live music on the park stage

    Acoustic sets under the oaks. Bring a layer; May shade is cool even when the vineyard rows are hot.

  • 2:00 PM – 4:00 PM

    🍇 Reserve pinot & Rhône flights

    Sta. Rita Hills pinot, Happy Canyon cabernet, and Rhône blends from Los Olivos houses. Ask for half-pours if you still want a Sunday vineyard tour.

  • 5:00 PM

    🍷 Last pour & park close

    Tents come down. Walk Mission Drive for a bakery box, then a designated-driver ride to Buellton or your inn. Do not leave a car on Copenhagen Drive overnight.

🌿 Sunday, May 9 — Vineyard add-on day

  • 9:00 AM – 11:00 AM

    🥐 Slow bakery morning

    Shorter lines than Saturday. Æbleskiver and coffee, then checkout. The park lawn will still have festival footprints — the village is yours again.

  • 11:00 AM – 4:00 PM

    🍇 Los Olivos or picnic winery loop

    Book a three-winery picnic or a walkable Grand Avenue tasting. Sunday is for vineyard context, not another park wristband.

Parking, pacing & May weather

Parking

Alisal Road edge lots and Mission Drive rear lots. Walk to Solvang Park. After 10:30 AM Saturday, hunting a Copenhagen Drive space is a tourist mistake.

☀️ May weather

Warm afternoons, cool oak shade. Hat, sunscreen, and a light layer for the 5:00 PM close. Hydrate between pinot pours.

Race weekend overlap

The Wine Country Marathon uses the same Saturday. Festival-only guests should arrive after 10:30 AM and skip the 6:00 AM start-line traffic.
