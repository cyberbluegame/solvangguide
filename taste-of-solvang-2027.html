What Taste of Solvang actually is

A compact March food-and-wine weekend — not a county-fair midway, and not the October valley-wide Taste of the Santa Ynez Valley.

Taste of Solvang has long been the village’s spring culinary weekend: chef walks, tasting-room trails, and bakery routes that treat Copenhagen Drive like a menu. The 2027 planning dates are Friday, March 19 through Sunday, March 21. Ticketed tastings and exact stage hours lock closer to winter 2026–27; use this page as the itinerary spine — Pacific Time, walkable, and built around village lodging plus valley wine add-ons.

If you already know Danish Days (September) and Julefest (November–January), this is the third pole of the year: smaller crowds than parade Saturday, better weather than December nights, and a reason to eat your way from Olsen’s to a pinot pour without leaving the village core. Pair a Friday check-in with a Saturday tasting trail and a Sunday bakery loop, then drive Los Olivos or Buellton if you still have room.

Full hour-by-hour schedule (always open)

No plus signs. Read the whole weekend. Times are Pacific Time and may shift when official 2027 tickets publish.

🍷 Friday, March 19 — Opening wine stroll

  • 3:00 PM

    🎟️ Check-in & wristbands

    Pick up stroll maps at Solvang Park or the Visitor Center. Wristbands unlock participating tasting rooms — do not start pouring before the listed open.

  • 4:00 PM – 7:00 PM

    🍷 Opening Wine Stroll

    Village tasting rooms pour Santa Ynez pinot, chardonnay, and Rhône blends. Walk Mission Drive to Copenhagen Drive. Spit cups exist for a reason if you are driving back to Buellton.

  • 7:30 PM

    🧀 First-night pairings

    Restaurant pop-up plates and cheese boards in midtown. Book a table or plan a Landsby / Corque dinner — Friday reservations vanish once the stroll sells out.

🥐 Saturday, March 20 — Pastry, chefs & wine trail

  • 10:00 AM – 12:00 PM

    🥐 Danish pastry walk

    Guided bakery route: æbleskiver, kringle, butter cookies, and seasonal tarts. Start at the east end of Copenhagen Drive so you finish near Solvang Park before the noon demo crowd.

  • 12:00 PM – 1:30 PM

    👨‍🍳 Chef demonstrations

    Local chefs plate valley produce with Santa Ynez wines. Stand on the north side of Solvang Park for a clear sightline. This is the Saturday photo hour — not the quiet hour.

  • 2:00 PM – 5:00 PM

    🍷 Tasting trail & gourmet dishes

    Restaurant and winery booths along the village core. Pace yourself: three small pours, one plate, water, then repeat. Designated-driver shuttles beat circling Alisal at 4:00 PM.

  • 6:00 PM – 8:30 PM

    🍷 Evening wine stroll

    Second-wave tasting rooms after the afternoon trail. Cooler March nights — bring a layer. Pair with a valley winery tour on Friday or Monday if you want vineyard context, not just village pours.

🇩🇰 Sunday, March 21 — Bakery routes & last pours

  • 9:00 AM – 11:30 AM

    🇩🇰 Solvang bakery tours

    Self-guided or ticketed bakery hop before the cases empty. Olsen’s and Solvang Bakery are the classic pair; add a third shop if you want kringle to take home.

  • 11:30 AM – 1:00 PM

    🧀 Cheese & wine closing flight

    Softer Sunday pours in the park or a reserved tasting room. Best window if you skipped Saturday’s evening stroll.

  • After 1:00 PM

    🎟️ Checkout & valley add-on

    Drive Los Olivos or book a picnic winery tour. Do not leave a car on Copenhagen Drive past checkout — enforcement tightens after festival teardown.

Bakery routes & pairing notes

A working pastry walk: coffee first, then æbleskiver or a sugar-dusted sphere, then kringle to share, then butter cookies in a tin. Save the heavy plate for the 2:00 PM trail. Danish architecture is the backdrop — half-timber and storks — not a costume overlay. If you only have one hour Sunday, do two bakeries and one tasting room, then get on Highway 246 before the checkout rush.

🧀 Gourmet pairings

Valley pinot with salmon or mushroom plates; chardonnay with pastry-adjacent cheese. Ask for half-pours on the Saturday trail.

👨‍🍳 Chef tables

Book Friday or Saturday dinner in the village. Midtown fills; Buellton is the overflow, not a last-minute plan after 6:00 PM.

🇩🇰 Village circuit

Windmills, Copenhagen Drive, bakeries, tasting rooms. Park once on Alisal. The festival is a walk, not a car crawl.

Parking, tickets & pacing

Parking

Alisal Road edge lots and Mission Drive rear lots. Walk. Saturday after 3:30 PM is the worst hour to hunt a space on Copenhagen Drive.

Tickets

Wristbands typically cover the stroll; chef tables and some dinners are separate. Buy the pass when it drops, not at the park gazebo at 3:55 PM Friday.

Pacing

Water between pours. One heavy plate at 2:00 PM. Bakery sugar is not a meal replacement before a wine trail.
