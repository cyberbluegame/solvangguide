Why this weekend fills Solvang

A Saturday race plus a park wine festival is two events sharing one village — plan parking and hotels as if it were Danish Days, not a quiet May morning.

The Santa Barbara Wine Country Half Marathon is the valley’s spring running weekend: oak-lined miles, vineyard edges, and a finish that dumps you back toward Solvang Park for partner pours. For 2027 the planning date is Saturday, May 8, with a Friday expo. Official start-line clock and wave times live on the race site once bibs open; treat 7:00 AM as the spectator arrival target unless your confirmation email says otherwise.

Spectators get more from the Alisal Road corridor and park finish than from trying to chase the course by car. Runners get more from a Thursday or Friday check-in than from a 5:30 AM drive up 101. The park festival is the reason non-runners book rooms — 🍇 local wine partners, shade under the oaks, and a village that is already dressed for a sunny May Saturday.

🎟️ Hotel deadline: lock walkable Solvang rooms by February on the hotel map below. Buellton is the backup, not a same-week surprise.

Fully open race-weekend schedule

Expo, start, course notes, and the Solvang Park wine festival — no collapsed cards.

👟 Friday, May 7 — Pre-race expo

  • 12:00 PM – 7:00 PM

    🎟️ Expo & bib pickup

    Downtown Solvang expo: bibs, shirts, partner booths. Pick up Friday. Saturday morning packet lines steal warm-up time you will want on Alisal.

  • 5:00 PM – 7:00 PM

    🍝 Carb-up in the village

    Book dinner early. Race weekend restaurants treat Friday like a holiday. Walk from your hotel; save the car for Sunday.

🏃‍♀️ Saturday, May 8 — Race start & course

  • 5:30 AM – 6:45 AM

    ☀️ Corral & bag drop

    Arrive early. Alisal Road is the parking story, not Copenhagen Drive. Layers off as the May sun comes up — valley mornings start cool.

  • ~7:00 AM

    🏅 Half marathon start

    Gun time is confirmed on your 2027 bib email. Waves move onto vineyard-adjacent roads and oak edges. Spectators: claim a curb on the outbound Alisal stretch, then walk to the park — do not try to leapfrog the course in a car.

  • 7:00 AM – 11:00 AM

    🌲 Vineyard route tips

    Expect sun, little shade after the first miles, and rolling valley grades — not a flat city 13.1. Carry what the race allows; aid stations are not tasting rooms. Watch for bikes and spectator drift at crossings.

🍇 Saturday afternoon — Solvang Park wine festival

  • 11:00 AM – 4:00 PM

    🍷 Solvang Park Wine Festival

    Post-race partner pours under the oaks. Finishers first; spectators with festival wristbands after. This is the May event non-runners actually came for — treat it like a ticketed tasting, not an open bar.

  • All afternoon

    🍇 Wine partner list (planning set)

    Expect Santa Ynez Valley pinot, chardonnay, and Rhône houses plus a rotating festival list. The 2027 printed map at the expo is the source of truth — this page is the logistics layer (park, shade, water, designated driver).

  • After 4:00 PM

    👟 Village cooldown

    Bakeries and Mission Drive reopen to the regular Saturday crowd. If you still want a sit-down tasting, book Los Olivos for Sunday, not 5:00 PM race day.

Spectator spots, itineraries & hotel deadlines

Best spectator spots: the Alisal outbound curb in the first two miles, a shaded oak edge if the course hugs the park, and the finish chute — then stay put for the wine festival. Chasing the course by car clogs the same roads runners need.

Weekend itinerary: Thursday or Friday arrival, expo Friday afternoon, early Saturday race or spectator curb, park festival, Sunday bakery and a slow checkout. Stack Taste of Solvang in March if you want two spring weekends; do not treat May 8 as a day trip from Los Angeles unless you like 4:00 AM alarms.

🌲 Course character

Oak hills and vineyard edges — scenic, exposed, and warmer by late morning. Sunscreen is not optional in May.

🏅 For runners

Friday bib. 5:30 AM lot. Shakeout walk Thursday. Do not invent a new breakfast on race morning in a packed bakery line.

🍷 For festival-only guests

Skip the 6:00 AM alarm. Arrive after 10:30 AM, park on Alisal, walk to the park, and leave a designated driver or use a shuttle.

Alisal parking & May weather

Alisal Road parking

Posted race lots and the Alisal edge. Walk to start and park. Copenhagen Drive after 6:30 AM Saturday is a tourist mistake, not a shortcut.

☀️ May weather

Cool start, warm finish. Arm sleeves you can drop. Spectators need shade and water at the park by noon.

Hotel deadlines

February for village inns. March if you will accept Buellton. April is leftovers and 101 traffic.
