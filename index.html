Frequently Asked Questions About Solvang

Everything you need to know before visiting the Danish Capital of America.

The best time to visit Solvang is during spring (March-May) and fall (September-November) when the weather is mild and the vineyards are most beautiful. Summer can be warm but perfect for wine tasting, while winter offers cozy Danish holiday decorations and fewer crowds.

Wine tasting in the Santa Ynez Valley typically ranges from $15-$40 per tasting room. Many tasting rooms waive the fee if you purchase bottles. Multi-winery passes are available for $50-$100 and offer better value for visitors planning to visit multiple locations.

Yes, Solvang offers ample free parking throughout the village. There are several public parking lots on Copenhagen Drive, Alisal Road, and Mission Drive. Street parking is also available and typically has a 2-hour limit in the busiest areas. Weekends can be busy, so arriving early is recommended.
