Solvang Danish Days 2027

Start planning for Solvang’s Danish heritage festival: folk dance, village traditions, Danish food, and a weekend of events in the walkable downtown.

Anticipated: September 17–19, 2027 · Downtown Solvang · Free admission · Dates and official schedule are not yet confirmed.

View the provisional schedule

Festival overview

Danish Days is a long-running celebration of Solvang’s Danish-American roots, with downtown activities, performances, food, and family-friendly traditions. The festival is free to attend (some extras, such as æbleskiver breakfasts, are ticketed) and is held on the third weekend of September, so the 2027 weekend is projected for September 17–19; organizers have not yet published final 2027 dates, times, or event details. This page is a planning guide, not an official event schedule.

Schedule details below are a sample planning timeline based on the festival’s usual format. Confirm dates, locations, admission, and hours with the official Solvang Danish Days organizers before making travel plans.
